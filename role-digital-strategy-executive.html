I'm not a trained developer, but I've never let that keep me at arm's length from the technology. I put in the work to understand how our platforms are actually built, so that I can get into the weeds with the team when a problem needs it, not just direct from a distance. My joy comes from the process of diagnosing a challenge, determining a course of action, creating a solution, and watching it work. And if it doesn't work, learning from it and getting better along the way.
