My career has really been two chapters: more than a decade in agencies, where my job was managing client relationships. I was the person accountable for multiple accounts, keeping projects on track, and making sure clients felt heard and well served. I've spent the past fifteen years inside one of the largest ministries in the US, where I lived the other side of that equation, leading marketing and digital operations. Through it all, I've stayed close to the work itself, building online stores and editing copy even while leading teams that designed and built websites and apps reaching millions each year.

Along the way I've managed marketing budgets up to $2 million, overseen a $30 million broadcast airtime budget, and grown social channels from a few thousand followers to millions. I hold a bachelor's degree in Marketing, with a concentration in advertising, from Georgia State University.
